_INVADERS

Waves of blocky aliens fly straight at you and you blast them before they land. Beginner mode is on by default, so you get an extra life while you learn the controls.

PlayAbout 0.1 MB to load the first time. Works with touch.
W A S D or arrows to moveMouse to look aroundClick, Enter or E to shootTouch: left half moves, right half looks, A fires

If the keys do nothing, click inside the game once. Click outside it to scroll the page again.

_INVADERS is by Andy K. S. Wong and is shared under the MIT license (read it). I host a copy here so it works on school Wi-Fi; the original is at github.com. Nothing from this game is saved to your kaleerson.com account. Co-op mode and the screenshot button are switched off in this copy because they need outside servers.
