Underrun

You get dropped into a station that went dark and have to reboot every system while robot spiders and sentries hunt you down. It's three levels, quick and tense, with a moody pixel look I really like.

PlayAbout 0.1 MB to load the first time. Needs a keyboard.
W A S D or arrows to moveMouse to aimClick and hold to shoot

If the keys do nothing, click inside the game once.

Underrun is by Dominic Szablewski and is shared under the MIT license (read it). I host a copy here so it works on school Wi-Fi; the original is at github.com. Nothing from this game is saved to your kaleerson.com account. The music runs on the Sonant-X synth, which is shared under the zlib license.
