How it works

You're red. Easy plays quick and a bit random, and has a Hint button that shows the column I'd pick. Hard looks five moves ahead, so plan your traps. Pass & play is two people on one phone. Beat Hard a few times in a row to climb the board.

A game you leave is saved on this device, so you can pick it back up. Starting a new game in the middle of a Hard game still resets your streak.
