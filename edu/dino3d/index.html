T-Rex Run 3D

It's the no-internet dinosaur from Chrome, rebuilt in voxel 3D with cacti, scorpions and pterodactyls. Press Space to start, jump the cacti, duck under the birds and see how far you get.

PlayAbout 6 MB to load the first time. Needs a keyboard.
Space / ↑ jump↓ / Ctrl duckSpace starts a run

If the keys do nothing, click inside the game once. Click outside it to scroll the page again.

T-Rex Run 3D is by Abraham Tugalov (Priler) and is shared under the GPL-3.0 license (read it). I host a copy here so it works on school Wi-Fi; the original is at github.com. Nothing from this game is saved to your kaleerson.com account. The start screen is a parody of Chrome's Russian offline page: «Начать игру» means Start game, and Space works too.
